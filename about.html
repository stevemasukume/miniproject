We make learning

The Campus Creative Club is a student society for curious people who learn through experiments, conversations and making things together.

01 / Our direction

Our mission

To give students a welcoming place to turn questions into action through collaborative, low-pressure creative practice.

Our vision

A campus where every student can find their people, try a new medium and make a useful contribution.

Our promise

Bring an open mind. Leave with a new skill, a new connection or a better question.
